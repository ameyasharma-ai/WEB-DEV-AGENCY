OUR STORY

Bringing Mumbai's Iconic Street Flavor to Mithapur, Patna

Urban Vada Pav was born out of a simple passion: to serve the authentic taste of Mumbai's bustling streets to food lovers in Patna without cutting corners. Located conveniently near Gauriamath Mandir in Mithapur, we take pride in our crispy vadas, secret spicy garlic chutneys, and warm hospitality.

Whether you are grabbing a quick evening snack after work, hanging out with friends, or ordering bulk snacks for a house party, Urban Vada Pav is your go-to destination for mouth-watering street food perfection.
