Our Story

Elevating Patna's Cafe Culture from the 5th Floor

Founded with a passion for great coffee and scenic hangouts, Figtree Cafe was born out of a desire to give Patna a true rooftop sanctuary. Perched on the 5th floor of Maharaja Kameshwar Complex opposite Buddha Smriti Park, our cafe provides an escape from the city's hustle and bustle.

Whether you're looking for a quiet morning workspace with an aromatic espresso, a cheerful afternoon meetup with friends, or a romantic evening under the sky overlooking Fraser Road, Figtree Cafe is your home away from home.
