The Story Behind Bauji Cafe

Founded with a vision to redefine casual dining in Patna, Bauji Cafe was born out of a deep love for community, soulful conversation, and exceptional food. Located strategically near Dashratha More on Bypass Road, we wanted to create a haven where friends and families could unwind away from the city rush.
