Our Story

Crafting Sweet Memories in Kankarbagh

Mardi Bakery and Cafe was born out of a simple philosophy: to bring authentic European baking standards and cozy cafe culture right to the heart of Kankarbagh, Patna.

Located near Gravity Mall in PC Colony, our cafe has quickly become a beloved neighborhood sanctuary for birthday celebrations, evening coffee dates, and delicious weekend treats. Whether you are ordering a custom photo cake or dropping in for a warm pastry, our team ensures every experience is delightful.
