Our Story

A Culinary Landmark on Fraser Road

Bansi Vihar Restaurant has been an integral part of Patna's culinary fabric for years. Located strategically near Dak Bunglow Chauraha on Fraser Road, we have welcomed families, business travelers, and food lovers seeking comforting, delicious pure vegetarian meals.

Whether you are stopping by for a hearty South Indian breakfast, a sumptuous North Indian lunch thali, or treating your family to evening sweets and snacks, our dedication to authentic flavors and warm hospitality remains unmatched.
