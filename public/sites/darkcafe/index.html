Our Story

Redefining Dining in Patna Since Day One

Nestled in the bustling Fraser Road Area of Old Jakkanpur, Patna, The Dark Cafe & Restaurant was born out of a desire to offer something truly distinctive. We noticed a lack of spaces that offered both mysterious, immersive ambiance and top-tier culinary standards.

Whether you are celebrating a special milestone, catching up with friends over coffee, or planning a romantic evening, our doors are open from 11 AM to 11 PM to serve you with warmth, style, and immaculate taste.
