Our Story

Where Good Food Meets Rooftop Vibes in Patna

Established with a passion for vibrant culinary experiences, Hot and Red Cafe and Restaurant has quickly become Patna’s favorite destination for friends, families, and couples alike. Located right at the bustling Boring Road crossing in Sri Krishna Puri, our 5th-floor rooftop setting offers an escape from the city hustle into a world of warmth, music, and exquisite flavors.

Whether you are planning a birthday celebration, a romantic candle-light dinner, or simply catching up over coffee and wood-fired pizzas, our dedicated team ensures every visit is memorable.
