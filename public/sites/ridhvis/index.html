Our Journey

Serving Happiness & Tradition at Atal Path

Established with a vision to redefine dining in Patna, Ridhvi's Restro has quickly become a beloved culinary landmark. Located conveniently at Atal Path, behind Vishweshwaraiyya Bhawan in MohanPur, Rajbansi Nagar, we take pride in offering an escape from the city hustle into a haven of delectable tastes.

Whether it is a joyful family celebration, a romantic candle-light dinner, or a quick evening catch-up with friends over snacks and mocktails, our welcoming ambiance and courteous staff ensure every moment is memorable.
