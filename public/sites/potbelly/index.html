Our Culinary Heritage

Where Art Meets Gastronomy in Patna

The Potbelly was born out of a deep passion for bringing authentic Bihari flavors to the global culinary map. Located within the serene and prestigious Bihar Museum complex on Bailey Road, our cafe offers a peaceful sanctuary where art enthusiasts and food lovers converge.

Every dish we serve tells a story of tradition, refined by contemporary techniques. Whether you are visiting the museum galleries or looking for a tranquil spot to work or unwind over specialty coffee, The Potbelly promises an unmatched experience.
