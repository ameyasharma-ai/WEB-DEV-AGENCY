Our Story

Brewing Conversations & Creating Memories in Patna

Founded with a passion for quality food and community, Cafe The Cartel was born out of a simple vision: to give Patna a world-class cafe where friends, families, and creators could gather in style.

Located strategically beside Vidyapati Bhawan in Lodipur, our cafe has rapidly grown into a cultural landmark for youth and food lovers alike. From our meticulously crafted burgers to our warm hospitality, every detail is engineered for perfection.
