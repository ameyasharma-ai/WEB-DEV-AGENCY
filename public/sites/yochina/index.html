Our Pioneering Heritage

Serving Patna's Best Chinese Since 2006

Established in 2006, Yo! China revolutionized casual dining in Patna. Located right at Bandar Bagicha on Fraser Road, we introduced food lovers to authentic oriental flavors, vibrant wok aromas, and an upbeat, welcoming dining ambience.

Over nearly two decades, we have served over a million happy customers. Whether it's a family celebration, a birthday party, or a quick dinner with friends, Yo! China remains Patna's most trusted destination for genuine Chinese cuisine.
