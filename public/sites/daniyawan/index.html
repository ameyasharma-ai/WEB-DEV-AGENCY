Our Story

Rooted in Tradition on Gola Road, Danapur

Located near St. Karens School on Gola Road, Daniyawan Fish Dhaba was born out of a profound passion for authentic Bihari non-vegetarian culinary heritage. What started as a modest roadside spot has grown into Danapur's most beloved destination for fish lovers.

We don’t use artificial shortcuts. Every single pot of fish curry features slow-cooked mustard paste, hand-crushed garlic, and farm-fresh spices that awaken your senses. Whether you dine in our vibrant setup or grab a quick takeaway via WhatsApp, we promise unbeatable flavor.
