Our Story

Redefining Fine Dining in the Heart of Patna

Situated at B-3, B-4 and B-5 on Fraser Road, Lodipur, SYZYGIUM was born out of a singular vision: to create a world-class culinary sanctuary where food lovers in Bihar could experience international dining standards paired with warm local hospitality.

Every corner of our restaurant is thoughtfully designed to offer an oasis of elegance. Whether you are unwinding after a busy workday, celebrating a milestone, or hosting an intimate dinner, our dedicated team ensures perfection on your plate and in your glass.
