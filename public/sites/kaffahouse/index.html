Our Story

Redefining Coffee Culture in Patna

Founded in the heart of Patliputra Colony, Kaffa House was born out of a profound love for authentic coffee and meaningful social gatherings. We wanted to create a sanctuary away from the city's hustle where coffee enthusiasts could savor world-class brews.

Whether you are catching up with an old friend, burying yourself in a good book, or holding a casual business meeting, Kaffa House offers the perfect blend of delectable flavors and tranquil ambiance.
