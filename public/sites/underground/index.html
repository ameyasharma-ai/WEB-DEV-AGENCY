Our Story & Philosophy

Redefining Patna’s Dining Culture One Plate at a Time

Founded with a passion for exceptional food and captivating atmosphere, The Underground Restaurant was born out of a desire to offer Patna a sanctuary away from the city hustle. Located in the heart of Boring Road at Tapasya & Chandra Kumud Complex, we blend gourmet culinary mastery with a cozy, clandestine vibe.

Whether it’s a celebratory family dinner, a romantic evening date, or a lively hangout with friends over sizzling starters and refreshing mocktails, our doors are always open to craft memorable dining moments for you.
