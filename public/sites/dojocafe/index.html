Our Story

Crafting Memorable Hangouts on Bailey Road

Founded with a passion for great taste and comforting spaces, The Dojo Cafe has quickly grown into Patna's premier destination for friends, couples, and food lovers. Situated conveniently on the 4th Floor of Bhavya Iconic Tower near Gola Road Crossing, we offer a serene escape from the bustling city noise.

Whether you are looking to unwind after a long day, catch up on work with high-speed Wi-Fi, or celebrate a birthday with delicious food and custom cakes, our doors are always open with a warm smile.
