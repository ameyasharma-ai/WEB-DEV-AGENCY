Our Story

Where Patna Meets the Stars

Located at the iconic SKU Centre on Boring Road (above Tasva Showroom, opposite Jyotipunj Hospital), Mood Light Cafe was born out of a passion to give Patna a world-class rooftop dining destination.

Whether you are planning a romantic candlelight dinner, a birthday celebration with close friends, or a peaceful evening unwind with great coffee, our 9th-floor sanctuary offers the ideal atmosphere.
