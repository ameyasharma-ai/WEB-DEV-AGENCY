Decades of Culinary Heritage

The Story of Chourasiya Ji Ki Purani Dukan

For generations, Chourasiya ji ki Purani Litti Dukan has been synonymous with the soul of Bihari cuisine in Patna. Situated near New Market at Pillar No. 6, our humble shop has served royalty, daily commuters, students, and food lovers from all corners of the globe.

Our recipe remains strictly uncompromised: hand-pounded sattu mixed with garlic, green chillies, black salt, carom seeds, and a splash of mustard oil, encased in wholesome whole wheat dough and roasted patiently over live charcoal.
