Our Story

Bringing Authentic Pizzeria Culture to Patna

Founded on Vardhman Road in Jakkanpur, Mithapur, Yo’z Pizza started with a simple belief: Patna deserved better than mass-produced, rubbery fast-food pizza.

We combined traditional dough fermentation techniques with high-grade local ingredients and bold spices tailored for Indian palates. Today, we are proud to be rated 4.7★ by over 164 passionate pizza lovers in the neighborhood!
