Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Modern Lounge Culture

Founded with a passion for soulful food and great conversations, Dhaba Cafe & Lounge was born out of a desire to give Patna a space where traditional highway dhaba flavors meet contemporary cafe comfort.

Whether you are dropping by after work for a quick mocktail, celebrating a birthday with friends, or enjoying a cozy family dinner, our warm hospitality and delectable dishes guarantee an unforgettable time.
