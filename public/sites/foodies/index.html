Our Culinary Journey

Bringing Patna’s Food Lovers Together Since 2021

Patna Foodies Club was born out of a simple love for good food and great company. Headquartered at Jaikriti Pearl Court in Kidwaipuri, our community has grown into Patna's most trusted culinary circle.

Whether it's savouring authentic Litti Chokha or tasting gourmet continental dishes at upscale cafes, we celebrate the diverse flavors that make Patna's food scene truly extraordinary.
