Our Story

Crafting Culinary Memories in the Heart of Patna

Nestled inside the prestigious Windsor Hotel on Exhibition Road, Bell Pepper Restaurant was founded with a singular vision: to elevate the dining standards of Patna by offering an exquisite fusion of traditional Indian gastronomy and contemporary continental delights.

Whether you are gathering with family for a celebratory weekend dinner, hosting a corporate lunch, or seeking a quiet, sophisticated corner for conversation, our dedicated team ensures every moment is accompanied by immaculate flavor and warm hospitality.
