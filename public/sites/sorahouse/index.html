Our Story

Where Patna’s Skyline Meets Warm Bohemian Hospitality

Perched high on the 7th floor opposite Gola Road at Rupaspur More, Sora House was born out of a desire to give Patna a peaceful, aesthetic, and vibrant escape above the city hustle.

Whether you are catching up with old friends over aromatic coffee, celebrating a milestone birthday under the open evening sky, or sharing a romantic sunset dinner, Sora House delivers unmatched ambiance, delectable cuisine, and soul-stirring music.
