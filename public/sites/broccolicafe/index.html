OUR STORY

A Haven of Taste & Warmth on Fraser Road

Broccoli Cafe was born out of a simple passion: to create a welcoming space in the heart of Patna where people could unwind over exceptional coffee and delicious comfort food.

Situated on the 1st floor of Photo House opposite Veena cinema hall, our cafe offers a peaceful escape from the bustling city streets. Whether you are catching up with old friends, planning a cozy date, or looking for a calm corner to work, Broccoli Cafe welcomes you with open arms.
