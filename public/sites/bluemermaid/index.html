Our Story

Crafting Memorable Dining Moments in Patna

Founded with a vision to bring a refreshing coastal-inspired vibe and metropolitan cafe culture to Kankarbagh, Blue Mermaid Cafe & Kitchen has rapidly become Patna's favorite gathering spot.
