Our Story

Where Great Food Meets Unforgettable Memories

Established in the heart of Kidwaipuri, Patna, Afra Tafri Cafe & Restaurant was born out of a passion for creating a lively, soulful space where food lovers could gather, relax, and savor exceptional flavors.

Conveniently located on the 1st Floor of Rai Complex on West Boring Canal Road (opp. Tata Motors, near Panchmukhi Mandir), we have grown to become Patna's landmark destination for birthdays, anniversary celebrations, cozy coffee dates, and joyful family dinners.
