Our Heritage Story

Serving Authentic Mithai with Passion in Rajbansi Nagar

Nandani Sweets was founded with a singular mission: to bring back the authentic, soul-satisfying taste of traditional Indian confectionery without any compromises on purity.

Located in the heart of Rajbansi Nagar behind Durga Mandir and near Haj Bhawan, we have become a cherished neighborhood landmark for families celebrating festivals, weddings, or simply craving an evening hot samosa with fresh sweets.
