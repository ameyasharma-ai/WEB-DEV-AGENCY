Our Story

A Culinary Landmark on Fraser Road Since 1940

Founded in 1940, Abhiruchi - Marwari Vaasa Bhojnalay began with a simple mission: to serve wholesome, nourishing, and deeply comforting vegetarian food that feels just like home.

Located right opposite the iconic Marwari Awas Complex on Fraser Road in Patna, our dining room has welcomed travelers, pilgrims, business professionals, and local residents for over eight decades. We continue to uphold the rich culinary traditions of Marwar with secret spice blends and uncompromising hospitality.
