Our Story

Serving Happiness & Great Food in the Heart of Patna

Banjara Boring Road was founded with a single mission: to provide food lovers in Patna with an unforgettable culinary destination. Located conveniently in Sri Krishna Puri, near Children's Park, we pride ourselves on blending authentic North Indian and multi-cuisine flavors with warm hospitality.
