Our Culinary Roots

Bringing Traditional Bihari Hospitality to Bihta

Founded with a passion to celebrate the rich culinary heritage of Bihar, Bihari Thaath has become a beloved culinary landmark in Bihta. Located right opposite the prestigious IIT Patna campus, we serve everyone from hardworking scholars to local families craving authentic, soulful food.

From the crackle of coal-roasted litti to the slow simmer of our signature handi curries, every dish tells a story of tradition, warmth, and uncompromised taste.
