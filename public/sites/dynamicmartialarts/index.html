Our Story

Empowering Patna Through Martial Arts & Discipline

Located in the heart of Machhua Toli, Patna, the Institute of Dynamic Martial Arts was founded with a singular mission: to impart authentic martial arts training that instills unbreakable mental focus, self-defense capability, and physical vitality.

Our academy provides a welcoming, high-energy environment for practitioners of all levels—from young kids taking their first martial arts steps to adults seeking rigorous self-defense and elite physical conditioning.
