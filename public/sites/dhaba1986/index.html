Our Heritage

A Culinary Journey That Began in 1986

Established with a passion for genuine North Indian hospitality and rustic flavors, Dhaba Estd 1986 Delhi, Patna has been a cornerstone of Patna’s food culture for over three decades. Located conveniently near Income Tax Chauraha in Kidwaipuri, we bring the soulful warmth of traditional highway dhabas right into the heart of the city.

Whether you are dropping in for a hearty family dinner, ordering comforting dal makhani for a cozy night in, or grabbing quick tandoori treats, every dish we serve carries the legacy of authentic spices and uncompromising taste.
