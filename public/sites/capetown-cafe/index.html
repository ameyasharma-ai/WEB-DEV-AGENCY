Our Story

Crafting Memories & Great Coffee in Patna

Founded with a passion for bringing world-class cafe culture to Patna, The Capetown Cafe has rapidly become the city's favorite sanctuary for coffee lovers, students, professionals, and families.

Located at Sumitra Palace on Atal Path, we pride ourselves on offering a serene escape from the hustle and bustle of city life. Whether you are catching up with an old friend, working remotely over high-speed Wi-Fi, or celebrating a birthday, our doors are open to make your day extraordinary.
