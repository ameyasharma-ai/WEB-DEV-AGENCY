Our Story & Hospitality

A Peaceful Oasis in the Heart of Kankarbagh

Founded with a passion for bringing world-class cafe culture to Patna, The Pacifique was designed to be more than just a place to eat. It’s a sanctuary where time slows down, conversations flow effortlessly, and every cup of coffee tells a story.

Located conveniently on PC Colony Road in Housing Board Colony, we welcome families, corporate teams, students, and couples alike. Whether you are seeking a quiet corner to read a book or a vibrant space to celebrate life's milestones, The Pacifique awaits you.
