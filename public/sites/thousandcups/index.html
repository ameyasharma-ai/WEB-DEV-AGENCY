Our Story

Rooted in Passion for Exceptional Coffee in Buddha Colony

A Thousand Cups was born out of a simple dream: to introduce Patna to a true specialty coffee culture. Situated right on Thana Road in Buddha Colony, our café has quickly become a beloved sanctuary for students, professionals, and families alike.

Whether you're looking to dive into a good book, hash out your next big startup idea over a hot pour-over, or unwind with friends over wood-fired pizza and artisanal shakes, our doors are always open with a warm smile.
