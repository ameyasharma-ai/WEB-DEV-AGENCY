Our Story

Serving Smiles & Crunch in Buddha Colony

Golpuri Cafe was born out of a simple passion: to elevate traditional Indian street chaats and golgappe into a clean, hygienic, and vibrant cafe experience in Patna.

Located conveniently at Chakaram More, right in front of Shanti Girls Hostel, we have quickly become the go-to evening spot for students, professionals, and food lovers looking for authentic chatpata flavors and cozy vibes.
