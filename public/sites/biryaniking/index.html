Our Story

Crafting Unforgettable Culinary Memories in Khagaul

Located in the heart of Jai Ram Bazar, Khagaul, Patna, Biryani King A Family Restaurant was founded with a singular passion: to serve authentic, soul-satisfying Dum Biryani and North Indian delicacies that bring families together.

Every handi of biryani we prepare tells a story of patience, premium aged basmati rice, hand-ground spices, and tender meats marinated to perfection. Whether you are dining in with your loved ones or ordering food at home, we promise an experience fit for royalty.
