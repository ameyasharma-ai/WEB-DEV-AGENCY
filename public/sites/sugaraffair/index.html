Meet Your Home Baker

Hi, I’m Indu! Baking Sweet Memories in Mithapur, Patna.

What started as a deep passion for baking sweet treats for family and friends has now blossomed into Sugar Affair by Indu. Located right near Laxmi Tower in Mithapur, Patna, my home bakery is built on the philosophy that every celebration deserves a cake that tastes just as magical as it looks.

Every sponge is whipped to airy perfection, every frosting is whipped fresh, and every custom design is crafted with meticulous attention to detail. Whether you need a midnight birthday surprise or an elegant anniversary tiered cake, I am here to make your moments sweeter!
