Our Story

Crafting Unforgettable Flavors in the Heart of Kankarbagh

Established with a passion for genuine charcoal-smoked cuisine, Koyla De Barbeque has grown to become Patna's most beloved barbecue destination. Located near the Rajendra Nagar Over Bridge in Kankarbagh, we bring families and food lovers together over sizzling platters and hearty laughter.

Our master chefs use secret spice blends handed down through generations, combining them with contemporary culinary techniques to ensure every bite delivers an unforgettable explosion of flavor.
