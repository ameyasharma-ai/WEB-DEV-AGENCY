Our Story

Serving the IIT Patna Community with Passion & Flavor

Located in the heart of IIT Patna, Bihta, Food Court was founded with a singular mission: to provide students, researchers, and faculty members with a cozy sanctuary offering delicious, hygienic, and affordable food.

From brainstorming sessions over cups of steaming tea to celebratory group dinners after project submissions, our food court has witnessed countless memories. We take pride in being more than just a dining spot—we are an integral part of campus life.
