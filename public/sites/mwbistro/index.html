Our Story

Patna's First & Finest Bus Cafe Experience

Founded with a vision to redefine casual dining in Patna, MW Bistro took an ordinary bus and transformed it into an extraordinary culinary destination. Located on P&M Mall Rd in Kurji, we've become the go-to spot for food lovers, college students, and families looking for something truly unique.

Whether you want to climb upstairs into our air-conditioned bus lounge, relax in our open-air seating area, or grab a quick burger on the go, our friendly team is always ready to serve you with a smile. Come for the bus concept, stay for the unforgettable flavors!
