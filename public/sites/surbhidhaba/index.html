The Surbhi Dhaba Story

Nestled right in the bustling heart of Patna at SP Verma Road, Surbhi Dhaba has been a beloved culinary landmark for families, students, and food enthusiasts alike.

What started as a humble dhaba with a passion for genuine North Indian spices has grown into a 4.0-star rated restaurant with over 1,415 verified happy reviews. Our master chefs grind fresh spices daily and fire up the tandoor at dawn to bring you smoky, buttery perfection in every single bite.
