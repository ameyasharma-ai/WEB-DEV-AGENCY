Our Story

Serving Love & Authentic Flavors on Exhibition Road

Located centrally on Exhibition Road (Old Jakkanpur, Patna), Vishakha Bhojnalay was founded with a singular mission: to provide wholesome, clean, and delicious home-style Indian meals to everyone away from home.

Whether you are a student preparing for competitive exams, a working professional looking for reliable daily tiffin, or a family seeking comforting traditional food, our kitchen delivers warmth and taste in every single bite.
