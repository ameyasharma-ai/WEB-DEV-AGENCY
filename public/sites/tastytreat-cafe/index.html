Our Story

Patna’s Favorite Destination for Food & Ambiance

Located near Panchmukhi Hanuman Mandir on Boring Canal Road, Anandpuri, Tasty Treat Restro & Cafe was born out of a simple dream: to give Patna a world-class rooftop dining experience where great food meets unforgettable conversations.

Whether you're celebrating a birthday, planning a cozy date night, or catching up with old friends over a sizzling platter, our chefs and hospitality team ensure every moment spent under our roof is pure magic.
