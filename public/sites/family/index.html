Our Story

A Passion for Great Food and Warm Hospitality in Patna

Located in the peaceful lanes of Buddha Colony, Patna, Family Restaurant was founded with a singular vision: to create a welcoming haven where families and food lovers can gather over wholesome, delicious meals.

From our humble beginnings to becoming a cherished local dining destination, we remain committed to uncompromising quality, authentic taste, and heartfelt service. Whether you are celebrating a milestone or enjoying a quiet Sunday dinner with your loved ones, our doors and hearts are always open.
