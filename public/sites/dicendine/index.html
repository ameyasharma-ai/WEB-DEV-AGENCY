Our Story & Vision

Where Good Food Meets Great Company

Founded with a passion for bringing people together away from screens, Cafe Network Dice n Dine was created to give Patna a vibrant social sanctuary. Located conveniently on Khagaul Road, Mithapur, we noticed a lack of spaces where people could enjoy delicious food while engaging in real face-to-face interaction.

Whether you are looking to conquer Catan, stack the Jenga tower, or simply sip on an artisanal shake while chatting with old friends, our doors are always open. Come for the food, stay for the games!
