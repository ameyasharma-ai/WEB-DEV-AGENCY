Our Story

Revolutionizing Recreational Sports in Patna

Located quietly off the bustling Sahdeo Mahto Marg in S.K. Puri, Boring Road, The Playground was born from a simple vision: to give Patna's youth, fitness enthusiasts, and sports communities a world-class arena where they can play safely, passionately, and professionally.

Opposite Indian Bank, our facility features top-tier turf maintenance, clean drinking water, seating galleries for spectators, and hassle-free booking via phone and WhatsApp. Join thousands of players who make The Playground their home ground every week!
