Our Sweet Story

Welcome to Cake Bagicha, Ramkrishan Nagar

Nestled on Jyotish Path right opposite Swastik Married Hall in West Ramkrishan Nagar, Cake Bagicha has quickly grown into Patna's most beloved neighborhood bakery. We believe that every celebration—big or small—deserves a centerpiece that tastes as extraordinary as it looks.

From fluffy sponge cakes layered with rich Belgian chocolate ganache to customized fondant masterpieces for birthdays and weddings, our team pours genuine passion into every batch. Drop by our store or order directly on WhatsApp for prompt delivery right to your doorstep.
