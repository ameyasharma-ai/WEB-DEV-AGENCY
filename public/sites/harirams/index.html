Our Heritage

Serving Happiness in Kankarbagh Since Years

Located near Bank of Baroda on Kanti Factory Road in Kankarbagh, Hariram's Sweets & Restaurant has grown into a beloved culinary landmark in Patna. What started as a passion for authentic mithai has evolved into a vibrant hub for families seeking delicious North Indian dining and festive treats.

Whether you are dropping by for an evening cup of tea with hot crispy samosas, celebrating a family milestone with our signature Kaju Katli, or ordering a grand thali for dinner, we pour love into every dish we serve.
