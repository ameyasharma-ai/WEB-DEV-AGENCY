Our Heritage & Story

Redefining Culinary Excellence in the Heart of Patna

Established with a passion for authentic flavors and superior hospitality, Regalia Restaurant has become Patna's preferred destination for families, food connoisseurs, and corporate diners. Located conveniently on Boring Road opposite A.N. College, our rooftop oasis offers breathtaking views paired with unforgettable culinary creations.

Whether you are craving smoky tandoori kebabs, rich Mughlai curries, or vibrant Chinese wok delicacies, every dish at Regalia is crafted using handpicked spices and fresh, locally sourced ingredients.
