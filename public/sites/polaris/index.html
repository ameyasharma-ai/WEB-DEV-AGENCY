Our Story

Two Brothers, One Vision: Hair Artistry at Its Finest

Founded by brothers Sumeet and Sujeet, Polaris Hair Studio was born from a shared obsession with the craft of hair. After honing their skills at world-renowned academies — Toni & Guy London, Vidal Sassoon, and L'Oréal Professionnel — they returned to Patna with a mission: to bring global standards of precision, color science, and hair health to Boring Road.

Today, Polaris stands as Patna's most awarded luxury salon, where every appointment begins with a thorough consultation and ends with a transformation that feels unmistakably you.

Frequently Asked

Your Questions, Answered

Do I need an appointment or are walk-ins welcome?

We strongly recommend booking ahead via WhatsApp to secure your preferred stylist and time slot. Walk-ins are accommodated based on availability, but wait times can be 30–60 minutes during peak hours (weekends & evenings).

Which color brands do you use? Are they ammonia-free?

We exclusively use Wella Professionals Koleston Perfect & Illumina Color, and L'Oréal Professionnel Majirel & Inoa (ammonia-free). Inoa delivers up to 100% gray coverage with zero ammonia odor and optimal scalp comfort. Olaplex bond protection is included in every color service at no extra charge.

What is the price range for a keratin smoothing treatment?

Keratin smoothing starts at ₹4,999 for short hair, ₹6,499 for medium, and ₹7,999 for long/thick hair. Price includes formaldehyde-free keratin, Olaplex prep, sulfate-free aftercare shampoo & mask (travel size), and a 2-week follow-up consultation. Results last 3–5 months with proper care.

Do you offer bridal & event packages?

Yes! Our Bridal Glow Package includes: trial session (hair + makeup), pre-wedding hair treatment, wedding day styling for bride & up to 3 family members, and emergency touch-up kit. Custom packages available for engagement, reception & festive events. Book 2–3 months in advance for best availability.

What safety & hygiene protocols do you follow?

Medical-grade UV sterilization for all tools, disposable capes/towels/gowns per client, HEPA-filtered air circulation, contactless payment & digital forms, mandatory hand sanitization at entry, and stations sanitized between every appointment. Your safety is non-negotiable.

Can I purchase the professional products you use?

Absolutely. We retail authentic Kerastase, Olaplex, Moroccanoil, L'Oréal Professionnel Serie Expert & Wella SP at studio prices. Your stylist will prescribe the exact regimen for your hair type after service. No guesswork, no counterfeit risk.
