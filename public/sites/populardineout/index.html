Our Story

Welcome to Popular Dineout Cafe & Restaurant

Nestled in A-1, Housing Board Colony, Kankarbagh, Popular Dineout has quickly grown into Patna's favorite culinary destination. Whether you're looking for a relaxing nook to sip coffee with friends or a vibrant restaurant for a hearty weekend dinner, we've got you covered.

Our master chefs bring together authentic spices, contemporary fast food recipes, and unmatched hospitality. Every dish is prepared fresh upon order to guarantee the highest quality.
