Our Story

Serving Passion & Tradition in Every Bite

Located at Satsang Bhawan Marg in Patna, Hungry Sardar was born out of a deep-rooted love for rich North Indian and tandoori cuisine. What started as a humble culinary dream has transformed into one of the city's favorite dining destinations.

We pride ourselves on sourcing the freshest local ingredients, maintaining rigorous kitchen hygiene, and infusing every dish with the warmth of traditional hospitality. Whether you're celebrating a milestone or craving comforting curries, we are here to make every meal special.
