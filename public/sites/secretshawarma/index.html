Our Story

The Quest for Patna’s Ultimate Shawarma

Secret Shawarma started with a simple passion: to bring authentic Middle Eastern shawarma flavors with an Indian street-food soul right to the heart of Kankarbagh, Patna.

Located conveniently in Housing Board Colony, we spent months perfecting our signature marinade and proprietary garlic sauce recipe. Today, with over 471+ glowing Google reviews and a loyal community of food lovers, we continue to serve the crunchiest, juiciest shawarmas in town.
