Our Story

Where Good Food Meets Great Memories in Kankarbagh

Pyne Square was born out of a passion to offer Patna a multi-faceted hospitality destination. Situated prominently on the 2nd floor of Parshu Villa near Gayatri Mandir Road, we blend a cozy cafe atmosphere with an upscale dining experience and a magnificent banquet hall.

With over 1,493+ glowing reviews from food lovers across Bihar, our commitment remains uncompromised: delicious dishes, impeccable hygiene, and warm hospitality that makes every guest feel like family.
