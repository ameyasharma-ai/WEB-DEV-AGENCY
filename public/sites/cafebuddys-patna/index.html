Our Story

Brewing Friendships & Memories in Patna

Cafe Buddy's Espresso started with a simple vision: to create a warm, inviting sanctuary where coffee lovers, students, and families can gather over exceptional brews and delicious continental treats.

Located at Narendra Villa right opposite St. Karen's High School on Gola Road, we take pride in serving aromatic espresso, comforting shakes, and wood-fired pizzas in an ambient atmosphere that feels like your second home.
