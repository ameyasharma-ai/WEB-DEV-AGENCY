Our Heritage

Bringing Families Together Through Good Food in Patna

Located in the heart of Patna at Adalatganj, Veerchand Patel Road Area, Ghar Aangan Restaurant was born out of a simple vision: to create a sanctuary where families and food enthusiasts could gather to enjoy wholesome, flavorful meals in an environment that feels just like home.

Over the years, we have served thousands of happy guests with dedication, hygiene, and authentic recipes. Whether it's a weekend family celebration, a casual lunch, or a cozy evening dinner, Ghar Aangan remains Patna's trusted destination for memorable dining.
