Our Culinary Narrative

Where Elegance Meets Exceptional Taste in Patna

Established in the heart of Kidwaipuri, Austaara - Fine Dine & Cafe was born out of a singular vision: to redefine the culinary landscape of Patna. We wanted to create a sanctuary where sophisticated ambiance harmonizes with uncompromising gourmet excellence.

Whether you are seeking a quiet corner for an artisanal coffee and book, an intimate candle-lit dinner, or a vibrant weekend celebration with family and friends, Austaara offers an unparalleled atmosphere paired with meticulously crafted multi-cuisine delicacies.
