Our Heritage & Story

The True Taste of Champaran Ahuna Mutton in Bihta

Originating from the Champaran district of Bihar, Ahuna meat preparation is not just cooking—it is a culinary masterpiece. At Bihta Champaran Meat House, we preserve this sacred technique where meat, garlic, onions, and raw mustard oil are mixed with spices, sealed tightly with wheat dough in an earthen pot (handi), and slow-cooked over wood fire.

Whether you are traveling along the Bihta-Arrah highway or looking for the best Sunday mutton feast with your family, our doors are open to serve you authentic, soul-satisfying food.
