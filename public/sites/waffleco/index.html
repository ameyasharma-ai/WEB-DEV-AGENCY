Our Sweet Journey

Crafting Memorable Dessert Moments in Patna

Located conveniently on Gola Road near S. K. Puram, Danapur, The Waffle Co. was born out of a passion for bringing authentic Belgian waffle culture to Patna. We noticed a craving for genuine, crispy-on-the-outside and fluffy-on-the-inside waffles paired with high-quality chocolate.
