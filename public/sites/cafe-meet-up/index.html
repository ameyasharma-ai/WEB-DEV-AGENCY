Our Story & Vision

The Ultimate Student & Friend Meetup Hub in Mithapur

Founded with a simple dream of creating a welcoming space where people can unwind, Cafe Meet-Up has quickly grown into the heart and soul of Mithapur, Patna. Situated right opposite NIFT College, we are the go-to spot for design students, college groups, and friends looking for warm conversations over hot coffee.

Whether you need a quiet corner to brainstorm your next college project, celebrate a birthday milestone, or simply relish a sizzling burger after classes, our doors are always open with a warm smile and freshly brewed aroma.
