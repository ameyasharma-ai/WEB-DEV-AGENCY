OUR STORY

Bringing Highway Soul to Patliputra Colony

Located right on Boring Patliputra Road, Cafe The Dhaba was born out of a simple vision: to give food enthusiasts in Patna a place where traditional dhaba heartiness meets cafe comfort.

Whether you are catching up with old friends over Kulhad Chai or treating your family to a lavish weekend dinner of butter chicken and tandoori kulchas, our kitchen promises uncompromising taste and genuine hospitality.
