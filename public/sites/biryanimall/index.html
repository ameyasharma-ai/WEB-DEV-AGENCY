Our Culinary Journey

Bringing Royal Awadhi Flavors to Fraser Road, Patna

Located near the Radio Station on Fraser Road, The Biryani Mall was founded with a singular mission: to serve authentic, soul-satisfying dum biryani that honors centuries-old culinary heritage.

Every single day, our master khansamas prepare fresh stocks of succulent meats and fragrant spices, sealing each handi with dough to preserve the rich aromas. With over 11,000+ happy customer reviews on Google, we are proud to be Patna's most trusted destination for true biryani lovers.
