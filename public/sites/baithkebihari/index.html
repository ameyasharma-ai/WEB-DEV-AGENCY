Our Story

The Concept of "Baithke" in Patna

"Baithke" means sitting together, sharing stories, and bonding over great food. We started Baithke Bihari Cafe with a simple vision: to create a warm, welcoming space in the heart of Patna where people can pause from their busy lives and enjoy genuine connections.

Located on the bustling Boring Road, our cafe offers a peaceful haven above the street noise. Whether you are catching up with old friends, studying, or on a coffee date, our ambiance and hospitality make every visit special.
