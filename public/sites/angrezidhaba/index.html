Our Heritage & Story

Redefining Highway Dhaba Culture with Urban Elegance

Founded with a passion to break culinary monotony, Angrezi Dhaba at Exhibition Road, Patna brought a revolutionary concept to Bihar. We took the soul of rustic Indian roadside dhabas and dressed them up in chic retro-modern aesthetics.

Whether you are dropping in for a family Sunday dinner or celebrating a milestone with friends, our kitchen serves up uncompromising hygiene, fresh ingredients, and unforgettable flavors.
