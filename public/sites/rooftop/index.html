Our Story

Patna’s Favorite Destination for Memorable Evenings

Nestled atop Bhavsagar Rai Palace near Gola Road Turning (behind Bharat Petrol Pump), Roof Top Cafe was born out of a passion to bring a world-class open-air dining experience to Patna.

Whether you are celebrating a birthday, planning a romantic candlelight dinner, or simply unwinding with friends after a long week, our rooftop provides the ultimate sanctuary of great food, stellar views, and warm hospitality.
