Our Story

Where Great Food Meets Patna's Skyline

Established on the 6th floor of KV Complex, above Silk House Hotel at RPS More, Kaliket Nagar, Cafe Skylite was born out of a passion to give Patna a truly world-class rooftop dining destination.

Whether you are planning a romantic candlelight dinner under the stars, celebrating a birthday with friends, or simply unwinding after a long work week with our signature mocktails, Cafe Skylite offers an unmatched ambiance paired with warm, attentive hospitality.
