Our Story

Welcoming Travelers & Food Lovers on NH30

Bihta Junction Restaurant was founded with a simple vision: to serve hearty, authentic, and hygienic food to anyone traveling along the busy NH30 corridor in Bihta, Bihar.

Whether you are driving down with your family or taking a quick break from a long business journey, our doors are open from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM every single day. Come for the tandoor specials, stay for the warmth.
