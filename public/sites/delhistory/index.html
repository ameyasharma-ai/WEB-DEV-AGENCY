Our Story

Bringing Delhi's Culinary Magic to Chajju Bagh, Patna

Founded with a passion for bringing metropolitan dining culture to Patna, The Delhi Story was born out of a desire to blend traditional North Indian heritage recipes with contemporary cafe aesthetics.

Situated conveniently on the 2nd floor of City Center Mall in Chajju Bagh, we provide a haven from the city bustle where food lovers can gather, relax, and savor moments over phenomenal food and drinks.
