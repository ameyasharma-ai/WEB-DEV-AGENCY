Our Story

Brewing Happiness in Patna Since Day One

Cafe Buddy's Espresso was born out of a simple passion: to bring a world-class coffeehouse experience to Gola Road, Patna. Whether you're a student looking for a post-school hangout spot, a working professional needing a caffeine boost, or friends catching up over shakes and pizzas, Buddy's is your home away from home.

Located at Narendra Villa right in front of St. Karen's High School, we take pride in our welcoming staff, warm ambient lighting, and consistently delicious menu. Drop by or order via WhatsApp to experience the magic yourself!
