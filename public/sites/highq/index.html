OUR STORY

Serving Happiness in Bihta, Patna

High-Q Food Court was founded with a simple vision: to bring a vibrant, hygienic, and diverse culinary experience to Bihta. Located strategically at Giri Baba Market near the Leather Factory, Ramnagar, we have quickly become the go-to destination for food lovers.

Whether you are looking for a quick evening snack after work, a celebratory family dinner, or a cozy place to hang out with friends, our dedicated team ensures every visit is memorable with delicious food and warm hospitality.
