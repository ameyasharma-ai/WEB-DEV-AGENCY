Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, check-in, and location.

New Pal Hotel is located right at Patna Junction in the Fraser Road Area. It is within a 2-5 minute walking distance from the railway station platforms, making it exceptionally convenient for train travelers.

We offer flexible 24/7 check-in and check-out services. Since train schedules vary widely, our front desk is operational around the clock to assist you upon arrival.

As per government regulations, all guests are required to present a valid photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport) at the time of check-in.
