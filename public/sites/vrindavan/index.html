Our Story & Heritage

Serving Tradition and Taste with Devotion in Patna

Vrindavan Kitchen was founded with a singular vision: to serve wholesome, soulful vegetarian food that reminds you of home-cooked meals while delivering restaurant-quality flavor and presentation.

Situated right across from the serene Buddha Smriti Park on Frazer Road, we have become a beloved destination for families, working professionals, and food enthusiasts seeking pure veg meals crafted with care.
