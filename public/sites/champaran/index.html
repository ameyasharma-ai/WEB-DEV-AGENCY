The Heritage Method

Why Old Champaran Meat House?

We adhere strictly to the authentic Champaran 'Ahuna' (also known as Handi meat) preparation style handed down through generations.

Slow-Cooked Earthenware

Meat and spices are sealed airtight in unbaked clay pots using wheat dough. Slow-cooked over gentle wood embers, locking in every drop of natural juice and aroma.

Farm-Fresh Whole Spices

We use pure unrefined mustard oil, generous portions of whole garlic bulbs, and hand-ground rustic spices sourced directly from trusted local farmers in Bihar.

Unmatched Hygiene & Taste

Backed by over 7,700 satisfied food enthusiasts on Google, our kitchen maintains rigorous cleanliness and consistent royal taste in every single handi.

Our Heritage Story

Bringing the Royal Flavours of Champaran to the Heart of Patna

Located strategically opposite Taramandal on Vidyapati Marg in Lodipur, Old Champaran Meat House was founded with a singular mission: to serve authentic, uncompromised Champaran Ahuna Handi meat to food lovers in Patna.

Unlike pressure-cooked commercial curries, our Ahuna meat takes hours of careful braising. The sealing of the pot allows the meat to tenderize in its own juices alongside roasted garlic and aromatic spices, creating a legendary flavor profile that keeps thousands of loyal customers coming back.
