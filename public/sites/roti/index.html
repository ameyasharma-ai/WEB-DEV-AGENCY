Our Story

Serving Happiness on Boring Road, Patna

Roti Restaurant has grown to become a beloved landmark for food connoisseurs across Patna. Located strategically opposite A N College on the bustling Boring Road, our establishment was founded with a singular mission: to serve wholesome, hygienic, and deliciously authentic Indian cuisine that feels like home.

Whether you are dropping by after college lectures, hosting a weekend family gathering, or ordering in for a cozy movie night, our dedicated culinary team ensures every single meal is prepared with passion and pristine quality.
