Our Story

Bringing Modern Cafe Culture & Authentic Flavors to Patna

Founded in the vibrant neighborhood of Hanuman Nagar, MG Cafe & Restro Swad-E-Bihar was born out of a simple passion: to create a welcoming space where food lovers in Patna could enjoy global cafe delights alongside authentic regional flavors.

Whether you are looking for a quiet corner to work with your laptop over a steaming cappuccino, celebrating a birthday with friends, or enjoying a hearty family dinner, our doors and hearts are always open.
